# Kastave V2 Media Scripts

Purpose: produce homepage assets that make visitors willing to leave an email and reserve the $1 founder credit.

## Target Audience Card Assets

These four images should feel like real U.S. bank-fishing situations, not generic product ads. Keep space for the website's animated overlay layers.

### A. The Bank-Only Bass Angler

Person:

- Adult U.S. bass angler, cap, polarized sunglasses, sun hoodie or fishing shirt.
- Standing on rocks or grass at the bank with a baitcaster or spinning rod.

Scene:

- Small lake or reservoir shoreline at golden hour.
- Kastave sits in reachable water close to shore, scanning the bank line.
- No boat, no dock electronics, no kayak.

Composition:

- Horizontal frame, angler on the left third, Kastave on the lower right third.
- Leave open water in the center for the animated scan route and two cast pins.

Overlay direction:

- Shoreline scan fan from Kastave.
- Two orange pins over reachable water.
- Thin route line following the bank.

### B. The After-Work Pond Hopper

Person:

- Adult angler arriving after work, casual outdoor clothes, compact tackle bag.
- Walking toward a neighborhood pond or public park water.

Scene:

- Late afternoon light, car or path in the background, short-session feeling.
- Kastave is already in the water near grass, scanning while the angler checks the spot.

Composition:

- Angler walking in the upper left or center-left.
- Kastave lower center or lower right.
- Leave upper right for a timer-style overlay.

Overlay direction:

- Clock ring in upper right.
- Three route dots showing quick scan progress.
- Soft green sweep line across the first reachable pocket.

### C. The Snag-Weary Lure Saver

Person:

- First-person or close bank perspective; hands and rod are enough.
- Show the lure path naturally, not a stiff posed full-body cast.

Scene:

- Calm surface water with visible shallow weeds, laydown, or submerged branch clues.
- Kastave does not need to appear in this shot.

Composition:

- First-person rod and line entering the water.
- Underwater branch, weeds, or snag hazard should be understandable through clear water or a subtle cutaway.
- Leave lower-right water area for warning markers.

Overlay direction:

- Orange warning triangles near submerged snag risk.
- A curved lure path that visually explains why the cast would hang up.
- Optional safe lane overlay, but keep it secondary.

### D. The Castable-Sonar Upgrade Seeker

Person:

- First-person view: one hand on fishing rod, one hand holding an iPhone-style phone.
- The angler is dealing with a round castable sonar on a fishing line.

Scene:

- Lake bank with clear open water.
- One round sonar ball hangs or floats on the line.
- Kastave is ahead and moving forward by itself, no line attached.

Composition:

- Phone in lower center/right showing a Kastave-style scan UI.
- Rod and sonar line enter from left.
- Kastave in middle distance moving away with a V-shaped wake.

Overlay direction:

- Dashed castable-sonar line on the left.
- Blue route and scan pulse around Kastave.
- App UI shows route line, scan fan, boat icon, and three cast pins.

## 1. Castable Sonar Hassle vs Kastave Auto Scan

Goal: show why repeatedly casting a round portable sonar is work, while Kastave scans by itself.

Shot list:

1. First-person shoreline view. Left hand holds the rod, right hand holds a modern phone.
2. One round black castable sonar ball hangs from the fishing line.
3. The angler casts or retrieves the sonar ball while checking the phone.
4. Kastave moves forward nearby with no line attached.
5. Show a V-shaped wake behind Kastave and open water ahead.
6. Phone screen shows the Kastave app: route line, scan fan, boat icon, and three cast pins.

Avoid:

- No multiple sonar balls.
- No visible competitor logo.
- No fishing line touching Kastave.
- No readable app text or fake legal claims.

## 2. Hidden Snag Under Calm Water

Goal: explain the pain point that the surface looks easy, but underwater is where lures get lost.

Shot list:

1. First-person bank-fishing view with hands on a baitcaster.
2. Water surface is calm and visually simple.
3. Fishing line enters the water naturally.
4. Cutaway or transparent underwater layer reveals a crankbait stuck on a submerged branch.
5. Keep the lure, branch, and line relationship easy to understand.

Avoid:

- No Kastave product in this shot.
- No awkward full-body casting pose.
- No dark muddy water that hides the lure.

## 3. Auto Scan to 3 Cast Calls

Goal: prove the Kastave workflow in one short sequence.

Shot list:

1. Hand places Kastave into the water from the bank.
2. Kastave runs a clean shoreline scan path.
3. App screen builds a dark lake map with contour lines.
4. Three pins appear: Safe, Structure, Risk / Reward.
5. Angler makes the first real cast after the scan.

Avoid:

- Do not make the app look like a generic map app.
- Do not show public spot sharing.
- Do not imply guaranteed fish catches.

## 4. Founder Reservation CTA Asset

Goal: support the conversion section.

Shot list:

1. Product carried by hand near a sunny lake edge.
2. Clean bright background with enough sky or water space for overlay copy.
3. Product stays visually consistent: black hull, olive green side panels, orange status light.
4. End frame leaves room for "$1 deposit / $100 credit" overlay.

Avoid:

- No dark background.
- No tiny product scale.
- No text baked into the image.
